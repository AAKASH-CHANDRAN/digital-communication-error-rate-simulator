6 Theory

What is BER?Bit Error Rate tells us what fraction of transmitted bits were received incorrectly.
What is channel noise?In this simplified model, noise is represented by the probability that a bit flips during transmission.

BER = Number of bit errors ÷ Total bits transmitted

BER (%) = BER × 100. This is a simplified binary bit-flip model, not a full radio-frequency or analog noise simulation.
